Patent Status Policy

THOR-SEC does not currently publish independent patent or patent-application numbers on this page. A public project is not described as patented, patent-pending, or filed unless a public identifier is available and its disclosure is intentional and appropriate.

This keeps the public research record precise and avoids conflating research novelty, defensive publication, internal invention review, and formal patent status.
